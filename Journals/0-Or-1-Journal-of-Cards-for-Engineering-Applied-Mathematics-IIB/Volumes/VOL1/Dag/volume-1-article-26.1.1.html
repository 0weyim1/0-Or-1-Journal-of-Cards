Engineering Applied Mathematics IIB for Two Players with Six Number of Jester Cards on Differential Calculus of Function of Several Variables


Dagnachew Jenber

Department of Mathematics, Addis Ababa Science and Technology University, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

Abstract:

This work presents 16 number of cards from applied mathematics IIB degree level mainly focused on differential calculus of function of several variables. The jester cards are
A. Functions of several variables, graphs, and level curves.
B. Limits and continuity.
C. Partial and higher-order derivatives.
D. Directional derivatives and gradients.
E. Total differentials, tangent planes, and the chain rule.
F. Relative and absolute extrema, including Lagrange multipliers.
